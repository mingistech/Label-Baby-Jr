# Label Baby Jr Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Label Baby Jr does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

## Data stored on your Mac

Label documents are stored only in locations you choose. App preferences and security-scoped bookmarks for recently opened label documents are stored locally in the app's sandbox container. Label Baby Jr does not upload the contents of your documents.

The Mac App Store edition receives updates through the Mac App Store and does not contact GitHub to check for updates. Choosing a Support or Privacy Policy command opens the corresponding public GitHub page in your default web browser.

## Printing

When you print a label, its contents are passed to the macOS printing system and the printer or printer driver you select. Label Baby Jr does not receive or retain information from the printer.

## Retention and deletion

You control the label documents you create and can delete them at any time using Finder. App preferences and recent-document bookmarks remain on your Mac until they are cleared by macOS or the app's sandbox container is removed.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, open an issue in the [Label Baby Jr support tracker](https://github.com/mingistech/Label-Baby-Jr/issues).

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the Mac App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the changed version is released.

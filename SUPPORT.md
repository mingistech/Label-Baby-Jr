# Label Baby Jr Support

Support is available through the public [Label Baby Jr issue tracker](https://github.com/mingistech/Label-Baby-Jr/issues).

When reporting a problem, please include:

- your macOS version;
- the Label Baby Jr version shown in the About window;
- the DYMO printer model, if the problem involves printing;
- a short description of what you expected and what happened.

Do not attach labels or screenshots containing private information. Label Baby Jr has no user accounts and does not collect diagnostic or analytics data automatically.
